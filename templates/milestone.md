> A milestone entry is not a title. It states what is in it, why it sits
> where it does, what done looks like as something measurable,
> dependencies, and risk. An entry missing the measurable-done line is not
> ready to be scheduled.

**What is in it.** {{The systems/content, concretely.}}

**Why it sits here.** {{What it unblocks; what had to land first.}}

**What done looks like, measurably.** {{The numbers/artifacts that will
exist when this is done — verdict keys with expected ranges, a frame
showing X, a count reaching Y. "The feature works" is not a done-state;
"`featureRuns=N>0` on three consecutive landings and the artifact-reader
confirms it in the noon frame" is. Write the instrument into the plan: if
the milestone's done-state cannot be measured yet, building that
instrument is the milestone's first item.}}

**Dependencies.** {{Milestones, decisions, fetches, owner input — named.}}

**Risk.** {{What could blow this up, and the cheapest early probe that
would surface it. A risk without a probe is a worry, not a plan.}}
